# The known differences between rucc and the reference GCC, each with the rucc issue that owns it.
# The columns are globs: row, flag set, key. check takes the first line that matches.

# The kernel flag sets: no FPU on x86_64 and aarch64, the riscv64 kernel -march, and the macros that follow.
*           kernel      *                                     tamnd/rucc#4096

# -m32 on the x86_64 targets.
# GCC gives the FreeBSD and NetBSD i386 defaults: 53-bit long double on FreeBSD, x87 math (FLT_EVAL_METHOD 2), -march=i386 without CAS, MMX or SSE.
# rucc gives the i686 SSE defaults of Linux.
*           m32*        *                                     tamnd/rucc#4088
*           boot-i386   *                                     tamnd/rucc#4088
